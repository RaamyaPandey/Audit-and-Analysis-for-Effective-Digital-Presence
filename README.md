🌐 Comprehensive Audit and Analysis for Effective Digital Presence

This project focuses on auditing, analyzing, and improving websites and landing pages to enhance brand visibility and digital marketing effectiveness. The case study is based on Hashmato (POS & Retail software brand) and includes my practical project RestroGrow (built on Wix).

🔑 Skills Learned

Digital Marketing Fundamentals

Website & SEO Audit

Landing Page Design (Wix, WordPress basics)

SEO Optimization (Titles, ALT text, Keywords)

User Experience & Mobile Responsiveness Testing

📊 Project Tasks

Company Research: Chose Hashmato and analyzed its website & digital presence.

Website Audit: Evaluated design, UX, loading speed, SEO, and responsiveness.

Digital Marketing Strategy: Suggested SEO blogging, Google/Meta ads, LinkedIn case studies, and traditional outreach like trade shows & referrals.

Landing Page Creation (RestroGrow):

Clean design layout

Added services, testimonials & contact form

Optimized SEO basics (titles, descriptions, ALT text)

Made it mobile-friendly
